# Preacher Discussion Checklist

Working checklist for Calvary Baptist Church website concept. Church leadership has not yet approved these decisions. Update this document after discussions.

## Decisions already made for prototype
- [x] Event RSVPs will be **optional for now**. Ask the preacher whether some events should require registration. (2026-11-18)
- [x] Keep the existing GitHub website as the design foundation; do not deploy to GitHub Pages yet.
- [x] Include a church events calendar. Do not invent actual events.
- [x] Preserve community-themed imagery from the earlier preview and incorporate real church photos when available.

## Events and RSVPs
- [ ] Should RSVPs stay optional or be required for selected events?
- [ ] Who can add, edit, and cancel events?
- [ ] Should the calendar include recurring weekly services or only special events?
- [ ] Who needs access to attendance totals and participant details?
- [ ] Should the church accept manual/in-person RSVP signups?
- [ ] What RSVP information is necessary (party size, adults/children, contact method), and who can access it?

## Church details and approvals
- [ ] Verify address, phone number, and service times.
- [ ] Approve doctrinal description, KJV language, and website copy.
- [ ] Supply church history, pastor biography, statement of faith.
- [ ] Confirm ministries, children's activities, and visitor information.
- [ ] Approve photos and permission to publish identifiable congregation members.

## Sermons
- [ ] How does the preacher currently record/upload sermons?
- [ ] Who should have permission to publish sermons?
- [ ] Video, audio, or both? Should messages be archived?

## Giving and communication
- [ ] Does the church want online giving? Which approved payment provider?
- [ ] Should the site offer contact forms and prayer requests?
- [ ] Who receives and responds to submitted messages?

## Ownership and launch
- [ ] Who will own and manage the church domain, hosting, and admin accounts?
- [ ] Who maintains the site after launch?
- [ ] Final church leadership approval before public launch.

## Website maintenance responsibilities — explain to preacher
- [ ] Explain **content maintenance**: updating calendar events, optional RSVP attendance, sermons, announcements, service times, church details, and approved photos. This should be manageable by a nontechnical church administrator once the editing interface is built.
- [ ] Explain **technical maintenance**: security and software updates, fixing bugs, hosting/domain renewals, backups, access management, and troubleshooting.
- [ ] Decide who is responsible for routine content updates and who serves as backup.
- [ ] Decide who handles technical maintenance, how issues will be reported, and what support expectations are reasonable for donated development.
- [ ] Clarify recurring costs (domain, hosting, storage, possible third-party services) and who pays them.
- [ ] Confirm church ownership of domain, hosting, data, and administrative accounts; agree on handoff if the volunteer developer becomes unavailable.
