About Calvary

A church family seeking to honor the Lord and remain faithful to His Word.

Our Faith and Practice

Calvary Baptist Church is an independent, conservative Baptist congregation in Trenton, Georgia. We use the King James Version of the Bible exclusively in teaching and worship.

We value Bible-centered preaching, prayer, reverent worship, fellowship, and sharing the gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our Church Culture

We hope visitors and members alike find a place to hear Scripture, build meaningful relationships, and encourage one another in their walk with Christ.

A formal statement of faith, church history, pastor biography, and ministry details await church leadership approval.
